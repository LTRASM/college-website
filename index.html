About Our Institution

A commitment to education and student learning.

Our Journey

Late Thakur Ram Autar Singh Mahavidyalaya was established in 2014. At the time of establishment, the college offered B.A. and B.Sc. programmes. D.El.Ed. was introduced in 2016, according to the information provided by the institution.

The college provides educational facilities including a library and a computer laboratory to support students in their studies.

College at a Glance

  • Established: 2014
  • Undergraduate programmes: B.A. and B.Sc.
  • Teacher education programme: D.El.Ed.
  • Library with study seating arrangements
  • Computer laboratory
